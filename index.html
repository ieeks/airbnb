1 Getting to the Apartment

You are arriving at Wien Hauptbahnhof (Vienna Central Station). From there, you have two options:

🚕 By Taxi / Uber / Bolt Tell the driver:
Aspangstraße 12/1, 1030 Wien

Journey takes about 5–10 minutes. Once dropped off, look for the building on the hill — the entrance is at the base of it, marked by the arrow in the photo below.
🚋 By Tram (Line 18) Head to the U1 metro entrance — the tram stop is right next to it.

Board Tram 18 towards Schlachthausgasse / Erdberg. Ride 4 stops and exit at the 5th stop: St. Marx.

Walk straight: pass DM and Billa Plus on your right → cross Fred-Zinnemann-Platz → take the short staircase up → Aspangstraße 12/1 is straight ahead.

The building as seen from the street — the entrance is at ground level, indicated by the arrow.

Arriving by tram: the staircase up from Fred-Zinnemann-Platz — the building entrance is ahead on your left.

🎟️ Buying a transit ticket

You need a valid ticket before boarding the tram. Two easy ways to buy one:

📱 WienMobil app — download it before you travel (free, iOS & Android). Create an account and buy your ticket in seconds. No cash needed, ticket is on your phone.

🖥️ Ticket machines — available at the tram stop and throughout Wien Hbf. Accept card and cash. Various time-based options are available — very handy if you're spending a few days in Vienna. If you're staying more than 3 days, the 7-day ticket is excellent value and covers all trams, U-Bahn and buses across the city.
